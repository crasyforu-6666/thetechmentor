3. Module 1: SAP Extended Warehouse Management (EWM)

SAP EWM remains the undisputed leader in enterprise logistics compensation. Unlike basic inventory management, EWM controls high-velocity warehouse robotics, automated conveyor integration (Material Flow Systems - MFS), Process-Oriented Storage Control (POSC), and complex wave picking strategies.

Why EWM Commands ₹20L–₹28L+ Packages:

  • Scarcity of True Functional Talent: While India has hundreds of thousands of standard MM or SD consultants, consultants who genuinely understand POSC customizing, Layout-Oriented Storage Control (LOSC), and RF scanner configuration are extremely rare.
  • FMCG, Retail & Pharma Boom: Companies like Amazon, Reliance Retail, Unilever, and Pfizer require real-time batch determination and zero-downtime warehouse automation.
  • Embedded S/4HANA Adoption: SAP discontinued standalone WM for S/4HANA migrations, forcing every enterprise to migrate directly to Embedded or Decentralized EWM.
🎯 MUST-KNOW INTERVIEW TOPICS IN EWM:

Warehouse Structure (Warehouse Number, Storage Type, Activity Area, Bins) • Storage Process Steps (IB01, IB02, IB03) • POSC vs LOSC customizing • Wave Templates & Release Profiles • Inbound Deconsolidation & Quality Inspection (QIE) • Physical Inventory in EWM (/SCWM/PI_EXEC).

4. Module 2: SAP Sourcing & Procurement (S/4HANA MM)

SAP Materials Management (MM) is the lifeblood of enterprise supply chains. In S/4HANA 2026, it is rebranded as S/4HANA Sourcing & Procurement. Every single SAP customer runs MM—without it, a company cannot buy materials, pay vendors, or track inventory valuation.

How MM Consultants Break Past the ₹20 LPA Ceiling:

Standard purchase order creators earn ₹6–8 LPA. But consultants who master:

  • Automatic Account Determination (OBYC): BSX, WRX, GBB, and PRD keys connecting MM to Financial Accounting.
  • Pricing Procedure (Calculation Schema): Access Sequences, Condition Types (PB00, FRA1), and Schema Determination.
  • Business Partner (BP) Synchronization: Customer/Vendor Integration (CVI) mandatory for all S/4HANA brownfield and greenfield migrations.
  • MRP Live on HANA: Execution via transaction MD01N with in-memory execution speed.

6. Module 4: SAP S/4HANA Finance (FICO & Central Finance)

Finance is the core metric of every CEO and CFO. In S/4HANA, the entire finance table architecture was condensed into the single Universal Journal (Table ACDOCA), removing the traditional barrier between General Ledger (FI) and Cost Center Accounting (CO).

Top earners in FICO specialize in Central Finance (CFIN), Parallel Ledgers (IFRS vs Local GAAP), and cross-module integration with MM (OBYC) and Sales & Distribution (VKOA).

7. Module 5: SAP BTP & Clean Core Extensibility

Under SAP’s strict Clean Core strategy for Cloud ERP, customers are prohibited from writing custom Z-tables and Z-programs inside the core ERP system. Instead, all enhancements must be built on SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP) using the ABAP RESTful Application Programming Model (RAP), Cloud Integration (CPI), and Event Mesh.

Consultants who can bridge functional requirements (in MM, EWM, or SD) with BTP integrations and SAP Joule AI agent workflows are commanding the highest contracting rates in Europe and North America.

9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Can a non-technical graduate or fresher really land a ₹15L–₹20L package in SAP?

Freshers who learn basic MM alone typically start around ₹4.5L to ₹7 LPA in Indian IT companies. However, freshers or lateral switchers (e.g. from mechanical, supply chain, or MBA finance) who demonstrate dual competency in both S/4HANA MM and EWM with verified hands-on server project practice routinely negotiate starting packages between ₹9L and ₹16 LPA, crossing ₹20 LPA within 2 to 3 years.

Q: Is ABAP coding required to learn SAP MM or EWM?

No. Both MM and EWM are 100% functional modules. You do not write ABAP code. Your job is business requirements gathering, SPRO customizing, Master Data configuration, and writing Functional Specifications (FS) for technical developers when custom logic is needed.

Q: Embedded EWM vs Decentralized EWM: Which one should I learn?

Start with Embedded EWM on S/4HANA. More than 80% of current client migrations use Embedded EWM due to reduced server licensing and direct transactional sync. Once you master Embedded EWM, understanding Decentralized EWM takes less than a week.
