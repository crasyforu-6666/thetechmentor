❓ People Also Ask: Google Search FAQs

Does Table BSEG still exist in SAP S/4HANA? ▼

Yes, BSEG still exists for document entry and open-item management, but ACDOCA is the primary reporting and analytics table containing all financial, controlling, asset, and material ledger dimensions.

What is an Extension Ledger in S/4HANA Finance? ▼

An Extension Ledger is a ledger that references an underlying base ledger (e.g. Leading Ledger 0L). It stores delta adjustments (such as management forecasts, tax adjustments, or simulated entries) without duplicating base data.

How does S/4HANA eliminate the need for the FI-CO reconciliation ledger? ▼

In S/4HANA, every internal controlling posting (such as cost center allocations) is simultaneously written as an ACDOCA entry with unified G/L accounts, ensuring FI and CO are always in 100% real-time balance.
