Mastering Material Master Views in S/4HANA: A Field-by-Field Deep Dive for MM & EWM Consultants

This comprehensive guide demystifies SAP S/4HANA Material Master views, providing a field-by-field explanation crucial for MM and EWM consultants in India.

Anshuman BehuriaLead SAP Mentor · theTechMentor
Updated Nov 13, 2026
ModuleSAP MM
Read time10 min
LevelIntermediate

As an SAP MM or EWM consultant in India, mastering the Material Master is non-negotiable. It is the backbone of logistics, procurement, warehouse management, and even financial processes in S/4HANA. However, many freshers and even experienced professionals struggle to understand the significance of each view and the critical fields within them, leading to configuration errors and operational roadblocks.

This article will demystify the Material Master views in S/4HANA, explained field by field, from creation to maintenance. We will go beyond just listing views, delving into the practical implications of key fields, their impact on downstream processes, and how they integrate across different SAP modules. By the end, you will have a solid understanding to confidently configure and troubleshoot Material Master data.

⚡ Key takeaways
  • Material Master views logically separate data for different business functions, ensuring data accuracy and process-specific maintenance.
  • Critical fields across Basic Data, Purchasing, MRP, Storage, WM, and Accounting views directly impact procurement, planning, warehousing, and financial processes.
  • In S/4HANA, the integration of Material Master data with EWM (via WM views) and Material Ledger (via Accounting views) is highly interdependent.
  • Incorrect configuration of fields like Base Unit of Measure, Price Control, Valuation Class, and MRP Type can lead to significant operational and financial errors.
  • Thorough understanding of each view and its key fields is crucial for effective configuration, troubleshooting, and seamless S/4HANA logistics operations.

01Why Material Master Views are Critical in S/4HANA Logistics

The Material Master (transaction code: MM01 for creation, MM02 for change, MM03 for display) is not a monolithic data object. Instead, it's logically segregated into various 'views,' each corresponding to a specific business function or department. This modular design ensures that only relevant data is maintained by the respective teams, promoting data accuracy and process efficiency.

In S/4HANA, the integration between modules is tighter than ever, especially with components like Embedded EWM. An incorrectly maintained field in one view can have ripple effects across your entire supply chain, from procurement (configure-purchase-order-cycle-s4hana.html) and production to sales and warehouse operations. Understanding these views individually, and how they interact, is paramount for any functional consultant.

While the fundamental structure of Material Master views largely remains consistent between SAP ECC and S/4HANA, the underlying tables and integration points, especially with components like Material Ledger and Embedded EWM, have seen significant enhancements. For instance, the tight coupling with Material Ledger (sap-s4hana-material-ledger-actual-costing-valuation-guide.html) in S/4HANA makes the Accounting views even more critical.

02Navigating Material Master Creation and Maintenance (T-codes: MM01, MM02, MM03)

When you create a new material using MM01, the system prompts you to select the relevant views. Your selection dictates which tabs will appear and which data you'll need to maintain. This initial choice is crucial, as adding views later can be cumbersome if you miss critical data dependencies.

For existing materials, you use MM02 to change data and MM03 to merely display it. Remember that some fields are at the client level (e.g., Basic Data), others at the plant level (e.g., MRP, WM), and some even at the storage location level. This hierarchical structure is tied directly to the SAP MM Organizational Structure.

Always select all relevant views upfront during MM01 creation. It saves time and prevents data inconsistency issues later. For example, if you foresee a material being used in production planning, select all MRP views alongside Basic Data, Purchasing, and Storage views.

03Basic Data Views (1 & 2): Core Material Attributes

These views, accessible under the 'Basic Data 1' and 'Basic Data 2' tabs, hold general information about the material that is valid across all organizational levels (client level). They are fundamental for any material master record.

Key Fields and Their Impact:

  • MARA-MATNR (Material Number): The unique identifier for the material. Can be externally or internally generated based on configuration in OMSL.
  • MARA-MEINS (Base Unit of Measure): The unit in which the material stock is managed. This is the primary unit for all inventory postings. Incorrect selection here can lead to major discrepancies in stock and valuation.
  • MARA-MATKL (Material Group): Used for grouping materials with similar characteristics, often for reporting and procurement analysis. Also key for account determination via OBYC.
  • MARA-BRGEW (Gross Weight) / MARA-NTGEW (Net Weight) / MARA-GEWEI (Weight Unit): Essential for transportation planning, warehouse slotting, and calculating freight costs.
  • MARA-MAKTX (Material Description): Short text describing the material. Crucial for user identification and printed documents.
  • MARA-EANNR (EAN/UPC) / MARA-EAN11 (International Article Number): Barcode information, vital for scanning in warehouse operations (like in EWM) and retail.
In an interview, you might be asked, 'What is the significance of the Base Unit of Measure (BUM) in the Material Master?' Your answer should highlight its role as the primary unit for all stock-keeping, valuation, and internal calculations, and how conversion factors to other units (like Order Unit) are defined relative to the BUM.

04Procurement-Specific Views: Purchasing, MRP 1-4

These views are critical for any material involved in the procurement and production planning cycles. They are maintained at the plant level (MARC table).

Purchasing View (MARC-WERKS):

This view (MARC table) is maintained for each plant where the material can be procured. It dictates how the material behaves in purchasing processes.

  • MARC-EKGRP (Purchasing Group): The key that identifies the buyer or group of buyers responsible for procuring the material. Used for reporting, authorization, and workflow.
  • MARC-BSTME (Order Unit): The unit in which the material is ordered from the vendor. A conversion factor to the Base Unit of Measure must be maintained.
  • MARC-MINBE (Minimum Lot Size) / MARC-MAXBE (Maximum Lot Size): Used in MRP to determine the minimum/maximum quantity to be ordered or produced.
  • MARC-KZKFG (Automatic PO): If flagged, allows for automatic creation of purchase orders from purchase requisitions (ME59N), requiring a valid info record.

MRP Views (1-4):

These views (MARC table) are essential for materials that are planned and controlled by Material Requirements Planning (MRP).

  • MARC-DISPO (MRP Controller): The person or group responsible for material planning. Crucial for managing exceptions and ensuring material availability.
  • MARC-DISMM (MRP Type): Defines the method used for planning the material (e.g., PD for deterministic MRP, VB for reorder point planning, ND for no planning). This is a foundational field for MRP execution (MRP Live).
  • MARC-DISLS (Lot Size Procedure): Determines how the system calculates the procurement quantity during MRP runs (e.g., EX for exact lot size, FX for fixed lot size).
  • MARC-FHORI (Scheduling Margin Key): Controls floats (release, production, after production) used in lead time scheduling for planned orders.
  • MARC-PRCTR (Profit Center): Important for internal controlling and segment reporting, derived from the material master.
Incorrect MRP Type or Lot Size configuration can lead to overstocking, stock-outs, or inefficient production runs. Always review these settings with the business process owners and production planning team.

05Storage and Warehouse Management Views (1 & 2): The EWM Connection

These views (MARD for Storage Location data, MLGN for Warehouse Number data, MLGT for Storage Type data) define how the material is stored and managed within a plant and, specifically, within an EWM-managed warehouse. They are paramount for seamless warehouse operations.

Storage Location View (MARD table):

This data is maintained at the plant and storage location level.

  • MARD-LGORT (Storage Location): The physical place where the material is stored within a plant.
  • MARD-MINBL (Min. Storage Bin Quantity): Used in inventory management to ensure a minimum quantity is always present in a bin.

Warehouse Management Views (1 & 2) (MLGN and MLGT tables):

These views become particularly relevant when integrating with SAP EWM (embedded-ewm-s4hana-setup-walkthrough.html). They are maintained for each warehouse number where the material is stored.

  • MLGN-LGNUM (Warehouse Number): The specific EWM warehouse where the material is managed.
  • MLGN-LGTYP (Storage Type): The default storage type where the material is expected to be placed or picked from. Critical for putaway and picking strategies (POSC/LOSC).
  • MLGN-KAPAZ (Capacity Usage): Defines how much space one unit of the material consumes. Used in EWM slotting and rearrangement.
  • MLGT-LETYP (Storage Unit Type): The type of storage unit (e.g., pallet, carton) associated with the material, influencing putaway and storage bin selection in EWM.
  • MLGT-KZRFU (Batch Management Requirement Indicator): For batch-managed materials (SAP MM Batch Management), this indicates if batches are required for warehouse management operations.
In S/4HANA, especially with embedded EWM, the integration between the Material Master's WM views and the EWM product master (which can be extended further) is very tight. Data maintained here directly influences EWM's ability to execute warehouse tasks efficiently.

06Accounting and Costing Views (1 & 2): Financial Impact

These views (MBEW table) are critical for material valuation and how the material's value flows into Financial Accounting (FI) and Controlling (CO). They are maintained at the valuation area (usually plant) level.

Key Fields and Their Impact:

  • MBEW-VPRSV (Price Control): Determines how the material is valuated. Standard Price (S) for internally produced materials or key raw materials, and Moving Average Price (V) for externally procured raw materials or trading goods. This has significant impact on financial reporting and inventory valuation (Split Valuation).
  • MBEW-VERPR (Standard Price) / MBEW-STPRS (Moving Average Price): The actual valuation price based on the selected price control.
  • MBEW-MLAST (Valuation Class): A crucial link between the Material Master and the General Ledger (GL) accounts. It determines which GL account is posted during goods movements (configured via OBYC).
  • MBEW-BWKEY (Valuation Area): Usually corresponds to the plant, defining the organizational level at which the material is valuated.
  • MBEW-XBEWJ (Material Ledger Activation): In S/4HANA, the Material Ledger is always active. This field indicates if actual costing is used for the material.
For materials requiring detailed cost analysis and actual costing, ensure the appropriate Price Control and Material Ledger settings are in place. This is especially relevant in S/4HANA's Universal Journal where all values are tracked with high granularity.

07Quality Management View: Ensuring Product Standards

This view (MARC table) is maintained for materials subject to quality inspection processes. It links the material to quality planning and control functionalities in SAP QM.

Key Fields and Their Impact:

  • MARC-INSMK (QM Proc. Act. for Matl): Controls the quality inspection process for the material (e.g., 'A' for inspection for goods receipt, 'B' for inspection for goods issue).
  • MARC-QMPFE (Inspection Interval): Specifies the interval in days for recurring inspections.
  • MARC-QMATN (Inspection Plan): Links the material to a specific inspection plan or group for quality checks.

Proper configuration here ensures that incoming goods or internally produced materials undergo necessary quality checks before being used or sold, crucial for maintaining product quality and customer satisfaction.

08Common Mistakes and Troubleshooting Material Master Issues

Material Master errors are among the most frequent issues faced by consultants. Here are some common problems and their solutions:

Common Error / IssueImpactSolution
Incorrect Base Unit of Measure (BUM)Inaccurate stock figures, valuation errors, issues with conversions.Change BUM via MM02 (if no stock/open documents) or create new material. For existing stock, complex material re-creation or stock transfer with unit conversion is required.
Missing Purchasing Group in Purchasing ViewCannot create Purchase Requisitions or Purchase Orders.Update via MM02. If existing documents are affected, may need to manually update them.
Valuation Class not assigned to G/L AccountGoods movements fail with 'Account determination for entry XXX not possible'.Configure OBYC for the relevant transaction key (e.g., WRX, GBB) and valuation class.
MRP Type misconfigured (e.g., 'ND' for planned material)Material not planned by MRP, leading to stock-outs or overstock.Correct MRP Type in MM02. Run MRP for affected materials immediately.
EWM-relevant material without WM viewsMaterial cannot be put away or picked in EWM, stock inconsistencies.Extend material to WM views via MM01 (selecting WM views) or MM02. Ensure Warehouse Number, Storage Type are correctly populated.
Price Control 'S' for highly volatile raw materialsSignificant price variances posted to variance accounts, misleading cost analysis.Switch to Price Control 'V' if appropriate, considering the impact on existing inventory and financial reporting. Often requires clearing stock.
A common interview scenario is: 'A user is trying to create a Purchase Order but gets an error that the material master is missing data. Which view would you check first, and why?' You should explain that you'd start with the Purchasing View to check for the Purchasing Group, Order Unit, and other relevant fields, as these are direct prerequisites for procurement.

09Interview Questions on Material Master Views

Understanding Material Master views is a fundamental skill tested in SAP MM and EWM interviews. Be prepared for questions that delve into both theoretical knowledge and practical application.

  • Explain the concept of Material Master views and why they are necessary in SAP.
  • What is the difference between client-level and plant-level data in the Material Master? Give examples of views for each.
  • Which views are essential for a material that will be procured, stored in an EWM warehouse, and planned by MRP?
  • Describe the significance of the Valuation Class and Price Control fields in the Accounting 1 view. How do they affect financial postings?
  • How does the Material Master integrate with SAP EWM? Name at least three key fields from the WM views that impact EWM operations.
  • What would happen if you maintained the Base Unit of Measure as 'PC' and the Order Unit as 'KG' without a conversion factor?
  • You are extending a material to a new plant. Which views are mandatory, and why?
  • What are the implications of setting the MRP Type to 'ND' for a critical raw material?
  • How can you ensure that a material undergoes quality inspection upon goods receipt? Which view and field are relevant?
  • Discuss a common Material Master error you've encountered and how you resolved it.

?Frequently asked questions

What is material master in S/4HANA?

The Material Master in S/4HANA is a central repository for all material-related information, serving as the foundation for logistics processes like procurement, inventory management, production, sales, and warehouse management. It stores data like material descriptions, units of measure, purchasing details, planning parameters, storage characteristics, and valuation data, organized into various logical views.

What are the material master views in SAP S/4HANA?

Material Master views in SAP S/4HANA are logical groupings of data fields relevant to specific business functions. Common views include Basic Data (1 & 2), Sales (Org. Data 1 & 2, General/Plant), Purchasing, Foreign Trade/Export, MRP (1-4), Work Scheduling, Plant Storage (1 & 2), Warehouse Management (1 & 2), Quality Management, Accounting (1 & 2), and Costing. Each view contains fields specific to its functional area.

Why are different views necessary for the material master?

Different views are necessary to manage the vast amount of data associated with a material efficiently and logically. They ensure that only relevant departments maintain specific data (e.g., Purchasing maintains purchasing data, QM maintains quality data), improving data accuracy, reducing redundancy, and streamlining maintenance. This modularity supports complex business processes and organizational structures within S/4HANA.

Which material master views are most important for SAP MM and EWM consultants?

For SAP MM consultants, the most critical views are Basic Data (1 & 2), Purchasing, MRP (1-4), Plant Storage (1 & 2), and Accounting (1 & 2). For EWM consultants, Basic Data (1 & 2), Plant Storage (1 & 2), and especially Warehouse Management (1 & 2) views are paramount, as they define how the material interacts with the advanced warehouse system.
