SAP S/4HANA Release Highlights: What Has Changed for MM & EWM Consultants?

AB
Anshuman Behuria Lead SAP S/4HANA Trainer
• ⏱️ 8 min read

As SAP continues to evolve the S/4HANA core, staying up-to-date with recent feature enhancements and deprecated legacy structures is crucial for consultants facing implementation milestones and client design workshops.

Key Architectural Innovations in S/4HANA

  • Fiori-First Procurement: New intuitive apps for Purchase Requisition management, Supplier Evaluation, and Real-Time Spend Analytics.
  • Embedded EWM Integration Simplification: Direct delivery integration without redundant CIF or qRFC overhead.
  • Machine Learning in MRP Live: Predictive lead times and auto-suggestions for preferred vendor sourcing.
