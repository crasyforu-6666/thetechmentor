Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how theTechMentor ("we", "us"), operated by Anshuman Behuria at youronementor.com, handles your personal data. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Details you give us: name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, and your answers on our application form (current role, timeline, budget readiness).
  • Payment details: payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive the payment ID, amount and the contact details you enter. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank credentials.
  • Usage data: pages visited, the ad or link you came from (UTM parameters), and device/browser information. This comes through cookies and similar tools such as the Meta Pixel and Google Analytics.

How we use it

  • To respond to your application and arrange strategy calls.
  • To deliver courses, live classes, server access and support you paid for.
  • To send course updates, batch announcements and relevant offers by email or WhatsApp. You can opt out at any time.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business: Razorpay (payments), Google (Workspace, Sheets, Analytics), Meta (advertising measurement), Zoom (live classes), YouTube (videos), Vercel (website hosting). We also share it when the law requires us to.

Cookies

We use cookies for analytics and to measure our ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work.

How long we keep it

Enquiry data is kept for up to 24 months after our last contact with you. Student and payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent to marketing. Email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards. No online service can be 100% secure.
