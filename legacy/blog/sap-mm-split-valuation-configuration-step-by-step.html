SAP MM Split Valuation Configuration Step by Step in S/4HANA

Configure split valuation in SAP MM from scratch: global and local valuation types and categories, material master setup, account determination, MIGO postings and common errors.

Updated Nov 17, 2026

T-codes covered

One material, one moving average price: that is the default in SAP. It stops working when the same material has genuinely different values, such as in-house produced versus bought-out, imported versus domestic, or a refurbished lot versus a new lot. Blend them into one price and your cost of goods sold, stock balance and margins all go wrong.

This guide walks through SAP MM split valuation configuration in the order you would do it on a project. It covers global valuation types and categories, local definitions per plant, material master setup, account determination, and MIGO postings. It also covers the errors you will hit and the questions interviewers ask. You should finish able to configure split valuation in a sandbox and explain the accounting behind it.

⚡ Key takeaways
  • Split valuation keeps one material number but values partial stocks separately, with one MBEW row per valuation type plus a header record.
  • Configuration has a global layer (types, categories, assignment) and a local layer (plant assignment, defaults, procurement attributes).
  • The valuation category is set in the Accounting 1 view; each valuation type then gets its own sub-material record, price control and valuation class.
  • Different valuation classes per type plus BSX entries in OBYC are what actually separate the G/L stock accounts.
  • Goods issues and transfers must name the valuation type; movement 309 moves stock between types.
  • Use it sparingly, because every type adds master data and period-end effort. Batch management is often the better tool for traceability.
Process at a glance

  2. 2Define global types and categoriesCreate types such as EXTERN and INHOUSE and assign them to a category.
  3. 3Assign locally to plantsSet which plants use the category, and the default and procurement attributes.
  4. 4Maintain material masterSet the valuation category, then create each sub-material in MM01.
  5. 5Set account determinationAssign valuation classes per type and maintain BSX in OBYC.

01What split valuation is and when to use it

Split valuation lets you keep one material number but value its stock in separate partial stocks. Each partial stock has its own quantity, price control, price and valuation class. The material master has a header material (the total) and one sub-material record for every valuation type.

The data sits in table MBEW, one row per valuation type. The header record has an empty BWTAR (valuation type) field and carries the valuation category in BWTTY. Sub-material rows have BWTAR filled.

Business caseValuation type exampleWhy split
Bought-out vs in-house madeEXTERN / INHOUSEDifferent cost: purchase price vs production cost
Domestic vs importedDOMESTIC / IMPORTImport duty and freight change landed cost
Quality gradeGRADE-A / GRADE-BDifferent selling value and stock account
New vs refurbishedNEW / REFURBRefurbished stock is valued lower
Batch-wiseBatch number as typeEach batch has its own price (see batch management)
Do not over-use it. Every valuation type multiplies material master records, price changes, period-end work and reconciliation effort. If the only difference is vendor or country, ask whether a separate material or a plain batch would be simpler.

02Global vs local settings: how the configuration is layered

Split valuation configuration has a global layer that is client-wide and a local layer that is specific to a valuation area. In most S/4HANA systems the valuation area is the plant. This is the part freshers most often get wrong.

LayerObjectPurpose
GlobalGlobal valuation typesMaster list of types (for example EXTERN, INHOUSE) usable in any plant
GlobalGlobal valuation categoriesGroups of types (category A = Purchased / In-house)
GlobalAssignment of types to categoriesDefines which types belong to each category
LocalValuation category assignments per plantWhich categories and types are allowed in a given plant
LocalDefault and procurement attributesWhich type is the default, and which type applies for external or internal procurement

Texts and the global master list are stored in T149D (valuation types) and T149 (valuation categories). The exact screens differ a little between releases, so treat the field names below as a guide and confirm them on your own system.

ECC vs S/4HANA: The configuration nodes are essentially the same. What changes in S/4HANA is the data behind it. Material Ledger is always active, and stock quantities and values come from the material document line items rather than from aggregates. See our Material Ledger guide for how that affects valuation reporting.

03Step-by-step SPRO configuration

Before starting, confirm that valuation is at plant level (OX14 shows the valuation level setting for the client). Split valuation is only supported when valuation is at plant level. Then work through the steps below.

The menu path is SPRO > IMG > Materials Management > Valuation and Account Assignment > Split Valuation.

#ActivitySample values
1Activate Split Valuation (client-wide switch, T-code OMWC)Tick the activation indicator
2Configure Split Valuation > Global TypesEXTERN = Bought-out, INHOUSE = In-house made
3Global CategoriesA = Procured vs produced
4Assign Global Types to Global CategoriesA → EXTERN, INHOUSE
5Local definition: assign categories to plantsPlant 1000 and 1100 use category A
6Set defaults and procurement attributesEXTERN for external procurement, INHOUSE for internal procurement
7Account determination in OBYCValuation classes per type, for example 3000 (raw material) and 7900 (semi-finished)
  1. Open OMWC and activate split valuation. Once you create valuation types for real materials, switching it off is not realistic.
  2. Define the global valuation types with short, meaningful codes. Codes are not translated, so use one naming convention for the whole landscape.
  3. Define a global valuation category and assign the types to it.
  4. In the local (plant-level) part of the same node, assign the categories to the plants that need them. Only those plants will accept the valuation category in the material master.
  5. Maintain the default type and the external and internal procurement indicators. These drive which partial stock a goods receipt from a purchase order or a production order lands in.
  6. Transport the settings. Treat them as cross-client-sensitive. A wrong global type reaches every plant.
Define the valuation types in a design workshop before you touch the system. Agree the codes, the owner of each, and the accounting treatment (separate stock account or not) with the FI team up front. Reworking types after stock exists is painful.

04Material master setup and price control

Configuration alone does nothing until the material master points to a valuation category. Create or change the material with MM01 or MM02, open the Accounting 1 view at plant level, and fill in Valuation Category (BWTTY). For field-level detail on this view, see our material master views guide.

  1. In MM01, select the Accounting 1 view and enter the plant. Set Valuation Category = A and save the header material.
  2. Run MM01 again for the same material. On the organisational levels dialog, enter the plant and the Valuation Type, for example EXTERN. This creates the first sub-material.
  3. Fill in the valuation class, price control and price for that type. Typically this is V (moving average) for bought-out and S (standard) for in-house made.
  4. Repeat for INHOUSE. Each type is a separate accounting record.

Remember what is where. Quantities are held per valuation type. The header material shows the total across all types, and its price is only a derived average for information.

Once valuation types exist for a material in a plant and stock has been posted, you cannot simply change the valuation category. Plan the category correctly at creation. If a change is truly needed, it normally needs zero stock and a controlled data-clean-up approach, tested in a sandbox first.

Price control can differ per type, but think about it before you do that. A standard-price in-house type and a moving-average purchased type is a common and sensible combination because production costs are revalued through variances while purchase prices float.

05Account determination for split valuated stock

The main reason to split value is usually to separate the stock on the balance sheet. That happens through the valuation class assigned to each valuation type in the Accounting 1 view. Because OBYC determines the stock account (BSX) from the valuation class, two valuation types with different valuation classes post to different G/L accounts. If they share a valuation class, they share an account, and the split is purely quantity and price. Our OBYC masterclass covers the full account determination logic.

Valuation typePrice controlValuation classStock account (BSX)
EXTERNV3000Raw material stock
INHOUSES7900Semi-finished stock

The valuation class must be allowed for the material type through the account category reference (OMSK). If the class is not linked to the reference for the material type, the system will not accept it in the Accounting 1 view.

Plan the valuation class for each type with FI before go-live. If a split is needed for management reporting only, use the same valuation class and let CKM3 or a custom report show the split instead of creating new G/L accounts.

06MIGO postings and how split valuation behaves

Goods movements must identify the partial stock. The valuation type gets into the document in one of three ways: you enter it, it comes from the purchase order or production order, or the system takes the default from configuration.

  1. Purchase order: in ME21N, the item carries a valuation type (field EKPO-BWTAR) on the material data tab. If the category is set and no type is entered, the default for external procurement is used. The full cycle is in our purchase order cycle guide.
  2. Goods receipt: in MIGO (movement type 101), the type is copied from the PO item. Stock and value post to the partial stock of that type.
  3. Goods issue: for a goods issue such as 201 or 261, you must name the valuation type to issue from. Entering the header material without a type is a typical user error.
  4. Transfer between types: use MIGO with transfer posting, material-to-material (movement 309), where the same material is both sender and receiver with different valuation types. If the types carry different prices or accounts, the posting creates the corresponding accounting lines. Test the exact accounts with your configuration in a sandbox rather than assuming.

To verify results, use MMBE for stock overview, MB51 for the material document list, FB03 for the accounting document, and CKM3 for the price and value history per valuation type.

After posting a test goods receipt, always open the accounting document and check three things: the valuation type on the line, the G/L account, and the amount. That tells you in two minutes whether OBYC and valuation classes are right.

07Common mistakes and troubleshooting

These are the problems that come up repeatedly in training batches and projects. Exact message wording varies by release and language, so match on the meaning.

SymptomLikely causeFix
Valuation category field is not accepted in the plantCategory not assigned to that plant in the local configurationAdd the plant to the local assignment and re-save the material
System asks for a valuation type, or says it is not defined for the materialSub-material was never created, or the wrong type was entered in the PO or MIGOCreate the sub-material record in MM01 for that type and plant
Goods issue posts to the wrong partial stockUser left the type blank and the default was usedTrain users, or make the type mandatory in the process and screen variants
Both types hit the same G/L accountSame valuation class on both typesAssign different valuation classes, and maintain BSX for each in OBYC
Valuation class not accepted for the materialClass not linked to the material type in OMSKAssign the account category reference to the class
Cannot change valuation categoryStock or valuation types already existClear stock and plan a controlled change; test in a sandbox first
Do not treat split valuation as a way to track quantity by lot. If you need traceability, expiry or quality status, use batch management, and use batch-level valuation only when each batch really has a different price.

A few more habits help. Keep type codes consistent across plants. Never create types with MM01 in production without the matching configuration transport. Revalue standard-price types through the controlled price-change process and not by editing prices ad hoc.

08Interview questions on this topic

Q: What is split valuation and why do companies use it? A: It values the same material in separate partial stocks, each with its own quantity, price and valuation class. It is used when origin, quality or batch makes the value different, for example in-house versus bought-out.
  1. What is the difference between a valuation category and a valuation type? The type is the individual partial stock (EXTERN). The category is the group of types allowed for a material, and it is the field you maintain in the material master.
  2. What is the difference between global and local configuration? Global defines the types and categories client-wide. Local controls where they are used, by plant, and which type is the default.
  3. Can two valuation types of the same material have different price control? Yes. Each type is its own accounting record, so for example one can be S and the other V.
  4. How do you separate the balance sheet accounts? Assign different valuation classes to the types, then maintain BSX for each class in OBYC.
  5. How do you move stock between two valuation types? Use a material-to-material transfer posting, movement 309, in MIGO.
  6. How does split valuation relate to batches? It can be done at batch level, where each batch is its own valuation type. Use it only when batches truly have different values.

?Frequently asked questions

What is split valuation in SAP MM?

It lets you value the same material in separate partial stocks, each with its own quantity, price control, price and valuation class. It is used when stock from different origins or qualities has different values.

What is the T-code to activate split valuation?

Activation is done with OMWC, or through SPRO under Materials Management > Valuation and Account Assignment > Split Valuation. The global and local definitions are maintained in the same configuration area.

Where do I set the valuation category for a material?

In the Accounting 1 view of the material master (MM01 or MM02) at plant level, in the Valuation Category field. The category must first be assigned to that plant in the local configuration.

Can I change the valuation category after posting stock?

Generally not in a simple way. Once valuation types and stock exist, the change needs a controlled approach, usually with zero stock. Test it in a sandbox before attempting it in production.

How do I post a goods receipt to a specific valuation type?

Enter the valuation type on the purchase order item, or let the default for external procurement apply. MIGO copies it into the goods receipt, and stock posts to that partial stock.

How do I move stock from one valuation type to another?

Use MIGO with a transfer posting, material to material, with movement type 309. The same material is both sender and receiver with different valuation types.

Does split valuation need separate G/L accounts?

No. Separate accounts only arise if the valuation types have different valuation classes and OBYC has different BSX accounts for those classes. With the same class, they share an account.

What is the difference between split valuation and batch management?

Batch management tracks lots for traceability, expiry and quality. Split valuation values stock separately. The two can be combined when each batch has its own price, but batch management alone does not split value.
