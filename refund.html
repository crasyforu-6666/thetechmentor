Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

No refunds on digital products

All theTechMentor courses — Recorded Sessions and Live Mentored Sessions — are digital products. Course videos, workbooks, server access and class links are made available to you as soon as you enrol. For this reason all sales are final and no refunds are provided once a payment is completed, whether or not you have started the course.

Please decide before you buy

To help you choose the right course, you can:

  • watch our free SAP video playlist and read the free guides on our blog;
  • book a free 15-minute counselling call with a career advisor;
  • ask us any question on WhatsApp or email before paying.

By completing a payment you confirm that you have read and accepted this policy.

Live Mentored Sessions

Live classes run in weekday and weekend batches. Your batch timing is confirmed at enrolment, and recordings of every live class are shared within 24 hours. Missing classes, changing your mind or a change in personal schedule does not make the fee refundable.

Recorded Sessions

Recorded courses are available 24/7 on any device, with lifetime access, from the moment you enrol.

Exceptions

  • Duplicate or failed payments: if you were charged twice for the same course, or money was deducted but the payment failed, the extra or failed amount is returned to your original payment method through Razorpay. Write to us with your Payment ID.
  • If we cannot deliver: if theTechMentor cancels a batch or is unable to provide the course you paid for, you may choose a full refund or a transfer to another batch or course.

Approved amounts are returned to your original payment method within 5–7 working days; your bank may take a few extra days to show the credit.
