Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Self-paced courses: 7-day money-back guarantee

If you are not satisfied, email or WhatsApp us within 7 days of payment with your Payment ID for a full refund, no questions asked. This does not apply if you have downloaded most of the course materials or shared your access.

Live batches

  • Cancel before the batch starts for a full refund.
  • Cancel within 7 days of payment and before the second live session for a full refund, minus any SAP server access already issued.
  • After that, fees are not refundable. You may move once, free of charge, to a later batch.

1-on-1 mentorship

Refundable in full within 7 days of payment if no mentorship session has taken place. Once sessions begin, unused sessions are refunded pro rata only if we are unable to deliver them.

If we cancel

If we cancel a batch or cannot deliver a program, you will receive a full refund or a transfer to another batch, as you choose.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to your original payment method through Razorpay within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few extra days to show the credit.
