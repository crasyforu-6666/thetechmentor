FAQ

Common questions

Do I need prior SAP experience?

Basic understanding of ERP or supply chain concepts helps, but we start from fundamentals. IT and logistics professionals with 0–2 years SAP exposure do well in our batches.

Which SAP version do you teach?

We train on SAP S/4HANA with focus on embedded EWM and modern MM processes. Concepts apply to ECC with noted differences where relevant.

Are classes live or recorded?

Live weekend sessions (Sat–Sun) with recordings available within 24 hours. You also get lifetime access to the recording library.

Do you provide placement assistance?

Yes — resume review, LinkedIn optimization, referral network access, and mock interviews are included. Placement is not guaranteed but strongly supported.

What is included in self-paced vs live?

Self-paced gives lifetime video access and workbooks at a lower price. Live adds weekend classes, SAP sandbox, mock interviews, and placement support. You can upgrade from self-paced to live with a credit.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Contact us after enrollment for EMI and corporate billing options for employer-sponsored learners.
