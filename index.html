FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need prior SAP experience?

No. A basic understanding of ERP or supply chain helps, but we start from fundamentals. IT and logistics professionals with 0–2 years of SAP exposure do well in our batches.

Which SAP version do you teach?

SAP S/4HANA, with a focus on embedded EWM and modern MM processes. We point out ECC differences where relevant.

Are classes live or recorded?

Live batches run on weekends (Sat–Sun) and recordings are available within 24 hours, with lifetime access. Self-paced courses are fully recorded.

Do you provide placement assistance?

Yes — resume review, LinkedIn optimisation, referral network access and mock interviews. Placement is strongly supported but not guaranteed.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, a theTechMentor certificate of completion. It is not an official SAP SE certification; we guide you on SAP's own certification exams if you want one.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. EMI and corporate billing are available for live batches and mentorship — ask your advisor after booking a counselling call.

What is your refund policy?

Self-paced courses have a 7-day money-back guarantee. Live batch refunds are explained in our refund policy.
